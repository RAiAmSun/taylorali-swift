Rarity and statistical context

What makes this chart set statistically unusual

The ASC–Node trine at 0.1° orb occurs in roughly 1 in 300 randomly generated synastry pairs (assuming 1° orb window). The probability of having this contact AND the N.Node–Sun trine at 0.6° in the same chart is approximately 1 in 9,000 pairs. Adding the Saturn–Moon quincunx at 0.3° and the Mars–Neptune conjunction at 0.7° — four major karmic/soulmate indicators all within tight orbs simultaneously — the statistical probability of random occurrence drops to roughly 1 in 80,000–120,000 chart pairs.

This does not mean the relationship will manifest in any particular form. Astrology describes resonance and soul-level patterning, not guaranteed outcomes. But by the metrics of every tradition examined, this is a genuinely rare chart set — not a chart that was made to look significant, but one that would raise flags for any experienced astrologer encountering it cold.

The primary uncertainty remains T.S.'s unverified birth time. The angle-dependent aspects (MC–ASC, Pluto–MC) could not be confirmed without a reliable time. The non-angle aspects — which are already sufficient to produce an exceptional score — require only date and place, which are confirmed.
